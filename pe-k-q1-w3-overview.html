Essential Question
Our Big Question
🤔
Think About This…
“Why is it important to stretch and flex our bodies, and how does it help us feel better and move better every day?”
🎯
Objectives
I Can Statements
1
I can name at least one part of my body that I can stretch or flex.
2
I can do a simple stretch and hold it for a few seconds without giving up.
3
I can describe how my body feels before and after I stretch.
👀
Before You Begin
Notice & Wonder

Do you notice that your muscles feel tight when you flex? Do you wonder why your muscles feel tight sometimes when you wake up in the morning?
